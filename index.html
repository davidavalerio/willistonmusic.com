About WMI

What

Multi-day workshops for pop instrumentalists & singers covering the performance skills they need to succeed: listening, practice techniques, basic theory, career advice, & more.

Who

WMI is for musicians high school-aged & above who want to hone their craft, whether they're gigging at bars, playing on their church worship team, or just wanting to explore their potential.

Why

Our region's musicians desire artistic community, training resources, & performance opportunities. Local players across bars, churches, & schools often haven't met one another—we want to change that.
