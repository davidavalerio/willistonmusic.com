Marketplace

Welcome to the Marketplace. Whether you’re a singer-songwriter looking for gigs, a band on the hunt for a bassist, or a business seeking entertainers to play at your next event, this is the place to connect.

Post a listing to find the people & opportunities you need, & we’ll post it once approved. Listings are free to submit & open to the Williston area (+75 miles). Open-ended listings stay up for 6 months unless you request removal (email team@willistonmusic.com). Dated listings come down after the event or deadline.

Note: WMI shares these listings as a free community service & does not screen, endorse, or guarantee any listing or the people who post them. Please use your own judgment when connecting with others.
